## Key bugs found while profiling (worth remembering)
1. **`target_cow` vs `target_cows`** — `_effective_target` looked up singular
   keys; cows were never placed/bought. One-line fix, −$20k when present.
2. **Wheat top-up capped at 8** — 14 animals eat 14/day → starvation → escapes.
3. **Planting happened at hour 16–23** (plant was the lowest priority) and a
   plant sown at hour 23 can never be watered → 62% of plantings died at age 1.
   Fixed with `plant_cutoff` (stop planting after hour 12) + earlier priority.
4. **Land bought too early** spread the farm so thin that reactive workers
   couldn't water everything → weed explosion (43 weeds). Fixed with a density
   gate (`land_density=0.60`).
5. **Priority order is load-bearing**: dig-high/plant-low keeps weeds down and
   the farm healthy. Reordering it cost ~$40k until reverted.
