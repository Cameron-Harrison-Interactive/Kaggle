Findings in the current bot
1. The packaged “brain” is not actually a trained decision model
HI_Market_Brain.pkl loads as a DecisionTreeClassifier with:

one tree node
one class: [1]
four features, all feature importances zero
It predicts 1 for every input. In practice, brain_sell() is an always-true vote whenever the price is at least 85% of the configured sell gate. It is not using learned market information. This is the clearest cleanup target, but it should be tested as an isolated A/B change because v5.8z5f’s score includes this behavior.

2. The bot delays ongoing-crop harvesting deliberately
For tomatoes and strawberries, it generally waits until yield_units >= 2 before harvesting, or until day 20. This batches sales and saves movement/actions, but it can lose production if a tile reaches its four-unit cap and remains unharvested. The rules confirm that ongoing crops are capped at four scheduled yields and decay. A safe experiment is not “harvest everything immediately”; it is a narrow rule that harvests when yield_units >= 3 or when the plant is at/near its decay point, then compares against 69,203.

3. Fertilizer and animals are intentionally unused
The rules confirm fertilizer can accelerate one-time crop yield but costs $100, while the current strategy is crop-first. Animals can be profitable only if the bot can reliably build, feed, care for, harvest, and sell them without stealing route capacity. This is a larger branch, not a safe small tweak to the champion.

4. Locked-tile movement is treated as blocked
route_step() avoids LOCKED tiles even though the official engine allows units to move across them. This is consistent with the bot’s deliberate “do not wander into unopened land” policy, so it is not automatically a bug. It does mean the route model is a strategy constraint rather than a rule requirement. Do not change this together with another pathing change.

5. The bot’s routing is intentionally rigid
Hired hands are assigned to fixed lanes and are told not to abandon their route. This avoids the failed v5.8z5f2 dynamic fence-row expansion, which caused workers to oscillate. The rigidity can leave an urgent water/harvest task waiting on one lane while another hand passes nearby. Any improvement should be a local emergency override only, with no mid-game route-map rewrite.

6. The crop constants need to be interpreted carefully
The bot uses maxday as the harvest target. The official rules say melon reaches its six-unit cap at age 10 under ordinary watering even though its listed max-yield window extends to age 12; fertilizer changes the daily bonus, not the maximum. The current later harvest is conservative and should not be changed without a controlled score test.

Best next experiments
Each should branch from the exact v5.8z5f package and be tested separately:

Brain-off A/B: set use_brain to 0, changing nothing else. This reveals whether the degenerate model helps or hurts.
Near-cap ongoing harvest: harvest ongoing crops at 3+ units, or when age is at/near their final scheduled yield, instead of waiting for 2/day-20. Keep routes unchanged.
Emergency route exception: if a crop has not been watered and is on the verge of its second missed day, let the nearest hand take it only when the hand is within a small Manhattan radius. Do not expand or remap routes.
Market-gate calibration: replace the global always-true brain vote with explicit per-crop price/quantity gates and compare against the current gates. This should be tested after the brain-off A/B, not at the same time.

Okay well we know our crops work and have a good amount of cash flow. We just need to figure out the animals So Animals

PLACE <item> [n] — Drop items from the active farmer/hand inventory into either a tile or the shed:

Animal placement: standing on a matching unoccupied structure (GOOSE on a coop, SHEEP/COW on a pasture) places one animal from inventory onto the tile. The n argument is ignored.
Shed drop: standing orthogonally adjacent to the shed moves up to n (default 1) of <item> from inventory into the shed. Capped by shedCapacity; excess stays in inventory.
FEED — Feed an animal using wheat (only needs to be done once per day)

HARVEST — Collect the eggs/milk/wool produced by the animal.

COLLECT_FERTILIZER — Collect 1 fertilizer from the animal. Every surviving animal makes 1 available at the end of each day, whether or not it was fed or cared for. Uncollected fertilizer does not accumulate, so an animal left alone for five days still yields 1 unit.

CARE — Care for an animal (once per day, no-op if already cared for). See animal care below.

Animal Care

CARE banks a yield bonus that is paid out on the animal's next scheduled production:

At end of day, if the animal was both fed AND cared for that day, pending_care_bonus increments by 1. Days where the animal was unfed do not bank a bonus (basic needs first).
On a scheduled production day, if the animal is fed, the entire banked bonus is added to that production's yield (in addition to the base 1) and the bank resets to 0.
If the animal is unfed on the production day, the base 1 unit is still produced, but the banked bonus is not applied and the bank resets to 0.
pending_care_bonus is capped indirectly by the per-animal max_held cap on yield_units.
Terrain

BUILD_COOP - adds a coop to an unoccupied tile
BUILD_PASTURE - add pasture to an unoccupied tile
DIG — Remove a plant from a square to free up space OR remove a weed from a square (does not yield any produce) OR remove an empty goose coop / pasture. A coop or pasture with an animal on it cannot be dug; the DIG is a no-op.
Other

PASS — Default if there is nothing to do (optional)
Market Action

Each turn you can submit up to maxMarketOrdersPerTurn (default 10) market actions; any orders past that limit are silently dropped. This is an ordered list and market orders will be processed in order simultaneously (one from each player) while both players have orders.

Watering / Animal Feed

Plants (and animals) must be watered/fed a minimum of every other day. Watering only needs to be done once per day, and subsequent watering actions are a no-op. In the case of plants not watered for two consecutive days, at the end of the day they turn into a WEED. In the case of animals they escape (unrecoverable).

A new seed starts with consecutive_unwatered = 1 — the planting day itself counts as the first missed day. A seed planted and left unwatered that same day reaches 2 at the end-of-day refresh and becomes a weed that night, before it grows. There is no grace period for fresh plantings.

A newly placed animal starts with consecutive_unfed = 0, so it survives its first day unfed.

Note that watering one-time yield plants during their yield window results in a higher yield. This is NOT true for ongoing yield plants/animals. See below.

Animal production branch design
Target: preserve the stable crop cash-flow engine while adding a livestock block that does not steal crop-route workers.

Ground-truth economics and rules
Goose: $300, egg every day, 4 held.
Cow: $400, milk every 2 days, 6 held.
Sheep: $500, wool every 3 days, 6 held.
Animals must be fed once per day. Two consecutive missed feed days causes escape.
CARE is optional for the first branch. It only banks a bonus for the next scheduled production and costs a worker action; feed and harvest are higher priority.
COLLECT_FERTILIZER is optional and lower priority than feed/harvest.
Animals require a matching structure. PLACE only works when standing on an unoccupied matching structure.
Animal products go into worker inventory and must be dropped at a shed-access tile before the market can sell them.
First serious animal branch
Use a fixed animal block in the starting/early unlocked area. Do not dynamically expand routes.

Proposed mix:

8 COW
4 SHEEP
12 fixed pasture tiles
4 dedicated animal hands, each responsible for 3 animals
remaining workers continue crop routes
no CARE in v1; add it only after feed/harvest is proven
buy wheat feed in bulk before the first animals are placed
do not buy an animal until a matching empty pasture exists
do not buy all animals in a single market burst if that crowds out seeds, hires, or land
Worker state machine
For each animal hand:

If carrying milk/wool/eggs/fertilizer, route to a shed-access tile and DROP.
If assigned animal is unfed today, feed it.
If its yield_units > 0, HARVEST.
If fertilizer is strategically enabled and available, collect it only after feed/harvest.
If feed stock is low, route to the shed and PICKUP WHEAT in a batch.
Move to the next assigned animal using a fixed non-overlapping loop.
The agent must never let an animal hand take a crop worker's route slot. Animal routes and crop routes are separate.

Opening sequence
Build pasture tiles first, using only the dedicated animal hands.
Buy/place animals only after structures are confirmed in the public farm state.
Keep enough cash for crop seeds and hires; do not spend the entire opening bank on animals.
The crop engine remains responsible for wheat/strawberry/melon cash flow.
Land purchases remain staged; no dynamic route rewrite.
Top-public-data clue
The public feature table's strongest rows are approximately:

peak crew: 12
first land: day 7
elbow day: day 14
tiles planted: ~131
crop counts: ~61 wheat, 45 strawberry, 23 melon, almost no carrots, no tomatoes
These are aggregate features and do not prove animal counts. They are useful constraints, not a substitute for the replay action sequence.

Validation gates
A branch is not eligible for activation unless it passes all of these:

no animal escapes
no animal misses feed for two consecutive days
crop bot remains above the stable crop-only baseline
animal products are actually harvested and sold
no worker is trapped oscillating between two tiles
final inventory does not contain dead unprocessed crops or animal products
validation is run against more than starter
